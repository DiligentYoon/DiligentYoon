## 👋 About Me

I research **learning-based control and safety control for legged robot systems**, connecting principled safety control theory with reinforcement learning, and sim2real transfer for hardware deployments in real worlds. My interests include **safe reinforcement learning**, **humanoid and legged robots**, and **safety-critical control**.

## 🔬 Research Focus

- **Safe Robot Learning** — Hamilton–Jacobi reachability analysis and safe reinforcement learning.
- **Humanoid & Legged Robots** — Whole-body control, locomotion, balancing, and sim-to-real transfer.
- **Safety-Critical Control** — Control barrier functions (CBFs/HOCBFs) and real-time safety filtering.
